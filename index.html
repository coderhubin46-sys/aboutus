About us

A menstrual solution for all

We believe that period care shouldn't be a source of anxiety or environmental waste. Our mission is to provide high-performance, skin-safe, and sustainable alternatives that empower every woman to live her life without interruptions. Through rigorous testing and thoughtful design, we've created the period care you actually deserve.

How it started

Born out of real frustration

It began with a simple question: why are we still using products that haven't fundamentally changed in decades? Products that leak, cause rashes, and fill up landfills.

Today, we aren't just selling a product; we're leading a movement toward conscious, comfortable, and chemical-free menstruation.

Save mother earth

Reducing environmental footprint with sustainable, reusable alternatives.
