Common Questions

What is FEMTTOSS Disposable Period Panty?
FEMTTOSS is a single-use, absorbent menstrual underwear designed to provide comfortable, convenient, and reliable period protection. It is worn like regular underwear and disposed of after use.
How does FEMTTOSS Disposable Period Panty work?
FEMTTOSS features an absorbent core that helps lock in menstrual fluid, providing leak-resistant protection and helping you stay comfortable and dry.
Who can use FEMTTOSS?
FEMTTOSS is suitable for teenagers, women, students, working professionals, travelers, and postpartum users when advised by a healthcare professional.
How long can I wear FEMTTOSS?
Wear time depends on your menstrual flow. Replace the period panty when it becomes saturated or uncomfortable. For good menstrual hygiene, change it regularly.
Can I wear FEMTTOSS overnight?
No. FEMTTOSS is designed for comfortable period protection. For very heavy flow, change it sooner as needed.
Is FEMTTOSS suitable for heavy flow?
FEMTTOSS can be used for light to heavy flow depending on the absorbency variant. Those with extremely heavy flow may need to change it more frequently.
Is FEMTTOSS available in different sizes?
Yes. FEMTTOSS is available in multiple sizes. Choose your size based on your waist and hip measurements or refer to our Size Guide.
Can I use FEMTTOSS while travelling or doing daily activities?
Yes. FEMTTOSS® is convenient for travel, work, college, walking, light exercise, outdoor activities, and everyday use.
Is FEMTTOSS reusable, and how should I dispose of it?
No. FEMTTOSS is designed for single use only. Roll and wrap the used period panty before placing it in a waste bin. Do not flush it down the toilet.
Where is FEMTTOSS manufactured?
FEMTTOSS is manufactured in India by Vipraved Ventures, Mysore, Karnataka, with a focus on comfortable and convenient menstrual care.
