About us

Designed for Comfort. Made for Confidence.

FEMTTOSS is more than a period-care product it represents comfort, convenience, confidence, and dignity during menstruation. With its focus on practical menstrual protection, FEMTTOSS looks forward to becoming a trusted choice for women across Karnataka and beyond.

With a focus on women’s changing needs and modern lifestyles, FEMTTOSS strives to make menstrual hygiene more accessible and comfortable. The brand also aims to encourage greater awareness around menstrual health and promote a positive approach to period care.

How it started

Born out of real frustration

It began with a simple question: why are we still using products that haven't fundamentally changed in decades? Products that leak, cause rashes, and fill up landfills.

Today, we aren't just selling a product; we're leading a movement toward conscious, comfortable, and chemical-free menstruation.

Common Questions

How do I wash my Femttoss panties?
Rinse with cold water after use until the water runs clear, then wash by hand or in a machine on a gentle cycle. Always air dry to maintain the integrity of the tech layers.
Are they completely leak-proof?
Yes! Our 4-layer technology is designed to absorb up to 6 tampons' worth of fluid while keeping you dry and safe from leaks throughout the day or night.
How often should I replace them?
With proper care, your Femttoss panties can last for over 50-100 washes, making them a sustainable choice for several years.
